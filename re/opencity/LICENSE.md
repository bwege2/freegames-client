# OpenCity: license check and replacement textures

Result of the first check (2026-11-15): OpenCity could not be ported as shipped, because two of its
textures contain photographs under Creative Commons **NonCommercial** licenses and under CC BY-SA 2.0,
which is not compatible with GPL-3.0. The site needs every asset to allow commercial use and to be
GPL-3.0 compatible (no NC, no ND).

**Operator's decision (2026-11-15): replace these two textures with free ones and port the game.**
The port (`web/games/opencity/`) ships the replacements described below; every other asset it uses is
the original's. The section "The assets that are not free" is kept as the record of why.

## The reference

**OpenCity 0.0.6.5stable** (2015), the last release (https://sourceforge.net/projects/opencity/),
as Debian distributes it: `opencity_0.0.6.5stable.orig.tar.bz2`, 8,385,950 bytes, sha-256
`7d7015bee0803f4b8257eefc5e1d7f437d581c6dcc0cd48628acf9896f0bc491` (`fetch.sh` downloads it and lists
the license notices below; `fetch.sh --assets` copies the data the port uses).

## The assets that are not free

Recorded in the models' own metadata (`<author>` in the `.xml`, comments in the `.conf`):

| Asset | Used as | Part | Photo | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `graphism/hospitaldept/large/large.png` (texture of `large.ac`) | **the hospital**: `OC_HOSPITAL_DEPT` = 370 in `config/graphism.xml`, the only hospital model of the game | brick texture | Pete Ashton, http://www.flickr.com/photos/peteashton/56656847/ | **CC BY-NC 2.0** (non-commercial) |
| same | same | air conditioning fan texture | Leo Reynolds, http://www.flickr.com/photos/lwr/6925865/ | **CC BY-NC-SA 2.0** (non-commercial) |
| same | same | window texture | Hans Voralberg, http://www.flickr.com/photos/hans_voralberg/112959208/ | CC BY-SA 2.0 (commercial use allowed, but not GPL-3.0 compatible) |
| `graphism/industrial/containers/containers.png` | industrial building "Shipping containers" (graphic code 35) | containers texture | Photocapy, http://www.flickr.com/photos/photocapy/173714279/ | CC BY-SA 2.0 (not GPL-3.0 compatible) |

The model files themselves call these textures GPL in their `license=` line (`large.conf`,
`containers.conf`), but the authors' notices next to it name the photos and their Creative Commons
licenses; the photographers' licenses take precedence. Only CC BY-SA **4.0** is one-way compatible with
GPL-3.0; the 2.0 versions are not. CC BY-NC and CC BY-NC-SA forbid commercial use outright.

## Replacement textures

Both replacements are option (a) of the operator's decision: textures we drew ourselves. They are
generated by `tools/make_textures.py` (Python with Pillow) from rectangles, ellipses and seeded noise; no
pixel of the original images is copied, traced or filtered. Only the layout is the original's: each part
sits in the same rectangle of the image as before, so the models' UV coordinates (`large.ac`,
`containers.ac`, unchanged) put the same part on the same face. Size and format are the originals'.

| File | Size | Source | Author | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `graphism/hospitaldept/large/large.png` | 512x512 RGBA | drawn by `re/opencity/tools/make_textures.py` (`hospital()`): left half a brick facade with sash windows on the original's 13x14 grid, the red cross on a white disc (top right of the facade), the glazed entrance and the air conditioning box with its round fan (bottom); right half the lawn with the two flat roofs, the bridge, the porch, the path to the street and the yellow helipad | the freegames project (2026), generated | **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication); may also be used under GPL-2.0-or-later |
| `graphism/industrial/containers/containers.png` | 256x256 RGBA | drawn by `re/opencity/tools/make_textures.py` (`containers()`): the 14 container door ends and 14 long sides in the original's rectangles and colours (blue, green, grey), with corrugation, doors, locking bars and corner posts; no lettering or company names | the freegames project (2026), generated | **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication); may also be used under GPL-2.0-or-later |

Differences from the photographs: the replacements are flat, drawn textures (no photographic detail),
the 632 transparent or half-transparent pixels of the original `large.png` (at least one in each row of
the facade half) are opaque in the replacement (the hospital model has no translucent object, so the game
draws that texture without blending either way), and the containers carry no shipping line names. Both are free for commercial
use and compatible with GPL-3.0 (CC0 is).
