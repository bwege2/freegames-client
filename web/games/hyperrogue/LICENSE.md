## This port

The TypeScript port in `src/` (a translation of the original's C++ source), the tools and oracle in
`re/hyperrogue/` and the tests are by the freegames project and are licensed **GPL-2.0-or-later** like the
original. It is a modified version of HyperRogue (`README.md` lists the state of the port and every deviation).
The website and game SDK are GPL-3.0-or-later with an additional permission under section 7 (`web/LICENSE.md`);
the game code and its assets keep their licenses above.

The port ships the original's fonts unchanged in `assets/fonts/` (stage 2): `DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf` (the
interface font) and `DejaVuSans.ttf` (DejaVu Fonts License, `assets/fonts/DejaVu Fonts License.txt`),
`cmunss.ttf` (SIL Open Font License, `assets/fonts/SIL Open Font License.txt`) and
`OpenDyslexic3-Regular.ttf` (SIL Open Font License 1.1, `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`). Stage 3 adds
`NotoSans-Regular.ttf` (Noto Sans, 17 MB, the font of the Chinese translation, © The Noto Project Authors, SIL
Open Font License 1.1, `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`; shipped unchanged and loaded only when Chinese or the Noto Sans
font setting needs it). `src/fontmetrics.ts` and `src/fontmetrics-noto.ts` hold metrics measured from
`DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf` and `NotoSans-Regular.ttf` (same licenses as the fonts).

The translations (stage 3) are the original's: `src/language-data.ts` and `src/lang/*.ts` are generated from
`language-pl.cpp`, `language-tr.cpp`, `language-cz.cpp`, `language-ru.cpp`, `language-de.cpp`,
`language-ptbr.cpp`, `language-fr.cpp` and `language-zh.cpp` of HyperRogue 13.1o (through the original's
langen.cpp), by Zeno Rogue and the translators those files name, GPL-2.0-or-later like the game.

The port ships the original's music and sound effects unchanged (stage 3), under their licenses above:
`assets/music/*.ogg` (the 17 tracks, CC BY-SA 3.0, by Shawn Parrotte, Will Savino and Lincoln Domina, credited
in `assets/hyperrogue-music.txt`, the original's music list, which the game also shows in its help screen) and
`assets/sounds/*.ogg` (CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY 3.0, CC BY 4.0 and CC0, credited in `assets/sounds/credits.txt`, the
original's file, shipped with them). The files are the original's, byte for byte; the port only plays them.
