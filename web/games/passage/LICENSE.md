## Favicon

The favicon of the game's page (`web/icons/passage.png`, 48x48, and `web/icons/passage-180.png`, 180x180; drawn by `web/scripts/icons.py`) shows the player and the spouse (the first frames of `characterSprite.png` and `spouseSprite.png`) under the full heart of `heart.png`, from Passage v4's source (Jason Rohrer, public domain), scaled up pixel for pixel on a rounded square.
