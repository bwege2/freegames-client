## This port

The TypeScript port in `src/` and the tools in `re/opencity/` are by the freegames project and are licensed
**GPL-2.0-or-later**, the same terms as the original. The website and game SDK are GPL-3.0-or-later with
an additional permission under section 7 (the full text is in `web/LICENSE.md`). The port is altered
source, not the original program.

The data in `assets/` is the original's, unchanged (`re/opencity/fetch.sh --assets`): `config/graphism.conf`,
the 79 models it lists (`.ac` AC3D files, their `.conf` properties and textures), the GUI images, the
terrain, water and cloud textures, the window icon and the seven sounds of `sound/sound.m3u` (GPL-2.0-or-later,
as part of the package), **except the two textures below**. `src/font8x8.ts` is the original's
`src/font_8x8.h`, the 8x8 console font of Linux 2.6.14 (GPL-2.0), converted by `re/opencity/tools/font.py`.
The original's height maps (`map/heightmap/`) are not used: four of them are CIAT SRTM data that may not be
redistributed commercially, and the game only reads them from its command line.

## Replacement textures

Two textures of the original contain photographs whose licenses do not allow this site to use them
(CC BY-NC 2.0, CC BY-NC-SA 2.0, CC BY-SA 2.0; see `re/opencity/LICENSE.md`). They are replaced, under the
same file names, sizes and UV layout, by textures drawn from scratch by `re/opencity/tools/make_textures.py`
(rectangles, ellipses and seeded noise; no pixel of the photographs is used):

| File | Size | Source | Author | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/graphism/hospitaldept/large/large.png` (the hospital, graphic code 370) | 512x512 | generated by `re/opencity/tools/make_textures.py` (`hospital()`): brick facade with windows, red cross, entrance, air conditioning fan; lawn, flat roofs, helipad | the freegames project, 2026 | CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication); also GPL-2.0-or-later |
| `assets/graphism/industrial/containers/containers.png` (shipping containers, graphic code 35) | 256x256 | generated by `re/opencity/tools/make_textures.py` (`containers()`): container doors and corrugated sides in blue, green and grey | the freegames project, 2026 | CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication); also GPL-2.0-or-later |

The photographs they replace were by Pete Ashton (bricks), Leo Reynolds (fan), Hans Voralberg (windows) and
Photocapy (containers); the models `large.ac` and `containers.ac` themselves are Frédéric Rodrigo's (GPL) and
are unchanged.

The GNU GPL version 2 is served as `licenses/GPL-2.0.txt` on this site and comes with the original's
`COPYING` in the reference download.

## Favicon

The favicon of the game's page (`web/icons/opencity.png`, 48x48, and `web/icons/opencity-180.png`, 180x180;
drawn by `web/scripts/icons.py`) is the original's window icon `graphism/icon/OpenCity32.png`
(GPL-2.0-or-later, above) on a green rounded square in the colour of the original's splash screen.
